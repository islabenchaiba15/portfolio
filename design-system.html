Color Palette

Almost entirely neutral — pure black, pure white, and one near-white gray for section separation. One warm accent (gold stars) and one cool accent (verified blue) used sparingly for trust signals only.

Typography

One geometric sans-serif throughout (Inter-style). Personality comes from weight and size contrast, not from mixing typefaces. Headlines are heavy (800) and tight (-2% letter spacing); body copy is regular weight and roomy.

Page Pattern (section-by-section order)

This is the structural skeleton you can reuse for your own one-page portfolio.

  1. Nav — logo, 2–3 links, utility icons.
  2. Hero — bold 2-line headline, one muted subline, single black pill CTA.
  3. Proof shot — full-width framed screenshot/mockup of your best work on a light gray band.
  4. Star rating strip — small social proof line under the proof shot.
  5. Product/Project detail — gallery thumbnails left, title + price/role + bullet specs + CTA right.
  6. Testimonial carousel — 3 cards visible, arrows to scroll, aggregate rating line above.
  7. Big statement — one oversized bold sentence (no image) as a pacing break.
  8. Feature/skill row — 3–4 icon columns explaining how you work.
  9. Secondary CTA band — repeat the hero CTA, shorter copy.
  10. FAQ accordion.
  11. Footer — 3–4 link columns + newsletter/contact field + socials + legal line.
Design system reference — built for reuse, not the original site. Swap copy, imagery and exact spacing to make it your own.
